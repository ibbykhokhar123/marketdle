## What was preserved

Every original company record and its order are preserved verbatim in `LEGACY_COMPANIES`. Existing puzzles through October 20, 2026 keep the old pool, values, chart reconstruction, scoring and seeds. Tests compare exact answers and response hashes for all 142 dates from launch through October 20, including all ten higher/lower rounds per date, ranking results, feedback, and trivia.

The expanded pool is usable in practice immediately. New daily puzzles begin using it on October 21, 2026 at midnight America/New_York. All original and added stocks are valid guesses, including in the existing daily Wordle and chart puzzle. Different-dated numeric snapshots are explicitly marked non-comparable instead of generating misleading directional clues.

No layout, CSS, colors, animation markup, component structure, routes, scoring rules, difficulty settings, localStorage keys, cookie logic, streak calculation or statistics data was changed. The only component edit extends the existing search-ranking function to recognize aliases and punctuation in share-class tickers; its complete UI markup and event handlers remain unchanged. Optional autocomplete aliases are additive; existing response keys and all legacy daily responses remain intact. New unavailable numeric fields use `null`, and unknown years use `N/A`, with compatibility guards. No player data was reset or migrated.

## Coverage and sources

The primary census is the full [Nasdaq stock screener](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/screener), downloaded from its public JSON endpoint with 6,986 securities. Each selected security was cross-checked against the [official Nasdaq-traded security directory](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/dynamic/SymDir/nasdaqtraded.txt) for its current symbol, listing exchange, ETF flag, and test-issue status. The directory covers Nasdaq, NYSE, NYSE American, NYSE Arca and Cboe listings; foreign companies with listed ADRs/ADS are included. OTC quotations, foreign-only listings, ETFs, preferred financing securities and debt notes are not separate operating-company entries.

The raw census contained 169 security rows over $100B: four were financing/note issues (CCZ, GOOGM, GOOGN, TBB), and two were second share classes of already-covered issuers (GOOG and BRK.A). The resulting 163 distinct issuers are all included. ITUB's listed ADR is included despite its underlying Brazilian preferred-share structure because it represents a distinct operating issuer, not an extra financing series of an already-covered issuer.

An independent cross-check of the first 400 global [CompaniesMarketCap rankings](https://companiesmarketcap.com/) found 164 over-$100B rows with confirmed U.S. exchange counterparts; all are covered. Its SK hynix domestic ticker maps to Nasdaq-listed SKHY; Berkshire's BRK-B spelling maps to BRK.B. Source values differ with timing and vendor methodology, so the larger population deliberately includes hundreds of companies below $100B as a buffer. The $100B threshold never removes an original or smaller company.

PLTR and MU have Nasdaq listings, sourced valuations, four-quarter financial totals, and actual historical closes. Their original listing years were checked against [Palantir investor relations](https://investors.palantir.com/news-details/2020/Palantir-to-Commence-Trading-on-NYSE-on-September-30-2020/) and [Micron's company timeline](https://www.micron.com/about/company/company-timeline). Nasdaq classifications conflicting with detailed company profiles for BAM and DPC were checked against [BAM](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bam/company/) and [DPC](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/dpc/company/); the sourced corrections and URLs are retained in `verified-facts.json`.

The 520 additions include 122 issuers over $100B, 279 between $10B and $100B, and 119 between $2B and $10B, across the source's sectors. All 48 originals remain, including smaller companies.

## Dates, metrics and historical data

The Nasdaq screener does **not supply a snapshot timestamp**. Values are therefore labeled as retrieved October 20, 2026, rather than claimed as an exact live close. The raw evidence, URLs, retrieval date and each company's financial period are retained. Some endpoints lag the latest session; this is disclosed rather than overwritten with a conflicting vendor quote.

Revenue and net income are sums of four actual consecutive quarterly statements, converted from Nasdaq's documented USD thousands to this app's USD billions. If four complete quarters are unavailable, values stay null. Where no direct P/E is supplied, a positive earnings multiple is calculated transparently as sourced market capitalization divided by sourced TTM net income. A loss or missing earnings leaves P/E unavailable. These are arithmetic derivations, not estimated missing inputs.

Employee counts and revenue-growth rates were not available from this import source. Additions leave them null and are excluded from those comparisons. The original 48 retain their existing June 2025 demo values for those metrics, labeled with their old date in higher/lower. New company valuations and other sourced metrics never overwrite the original historical records; a separate overlay is used for future puzzles.

New price series use only dated Nasdaq closes, in chronological order. No artificial anchors, random filler, backfilled pre-IPO prices or invented missing-day values were added. A shorter listing supplies only its available history. Nasdaq closes are retained as delivered; corporate-action adjustments have not been independently audited for every symbol. VYLR has no usable source history, so it remains a valid stock/guess and comparison candidate but is not selected as a mystery chart. Its Wordle chart hint reports the missing history.

The existing site's demo branding and visual notices were retained as requested. Per-game source dates and reveal source notes distinguish new records from the original demo figures; this work does not claim to convert every legacy fixture into verified live data.
